# Task 11

## Importance of Embeddings

Embeddings convert text into numerical vectors while preserving semantic meaning.

## Why Vector Databases?

Vector databases efficiently store and retrieve embeddings using similarity search.

## Relation to RAG

RAG systems retrieve relevant chunks from vector databases and provide them to LLMs for answering queries.
