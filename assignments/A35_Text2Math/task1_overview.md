# Task 1: Text-to-Math Agent Overview

## 1. What is a Text-to-Math problem?

A Text-to-Math problem is a natural language question that requires mathematical reasoning and calculations to find an answer.

Example:

"If a product costs ₹500 and receives a 20% discount, what is the final price?"

The AI must understand the text, extract numbers, perform calculations, and provide the result.

---

## 2. Why are agents useful for math reasoning?

Agents are useful because they can:

* Break complex problems into smaller steps.
* Decide when calculations are required.
* Use external tools such as calculators.
* Reduce arithmetic mistakes.

---

## 3. Difference between normal LLM response and agent-based reasoning

### Normal LLM

* Answers directly from learned patterns.
* May perform calculations incorrectly.
* Does not use external tools.

### Agent-Based Reasoning

* Analyzes the problem.
* Chooses tools automatically.
* Performs accurate calculations.
* Produces more reliable mathematical answers.
